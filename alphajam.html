Summary

AlphaJam is a relatively small quality-of-life mod for Alpha 1.2.6 that has been made with no particular goal in mind other than to improve the overall experience. This mod does NOT alter the core game mechanics in any way except for fixing major bugs present in the version, such as ore generation and leaf decay.

This mod is made for people who still want to play Alpha mostly as-is but with some improvements. Also this is NOT a recreation of legacy console edition. It has a few features from LCE, but that is not the primary goal.

By the way, if you don't trust the download button, AlphaJam is also available on modrinth here.
